### **Developing People and Teams**

### 1. What is the primary responsibility of a self-managing team in Scrum?

- [ ] **A)** To assign tasks to individual team members
- [ ] **B)** To decide how to perform the work within the Sprint Goal
- [ ] **C)** To report progress daily to the Scrum Master
- [ ] **D)** To define the order of the Product Backlog

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: B**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> A self-managing team determines how to perform its work within the Sprint Goal, not who does what individually.
 
 
</details>

### 2. Which two statements are true about cross-functional teams in Scrum?

- [ ] **A)** Every team member must be an expert in all skills
- [ ] **B)** The team collectively has all necessary skills to deliver a potentially releasable Increment
- [ ] **C)** The team relies on external specialists to complete tasks
- [ ] **D)** Cross-functionality includes having skills such as development, testing, and UX design

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: B, D**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> A cross-functional team possesses all required skills collectively; it doesn't require every member to be an expert in everything.
 
 
</details>

### 8. Which two techniques help a Scrum Master coach a team toward self-management?

### **Managing Products with Agility**

### 11. What is the product vision in the context of Scrum?

- [ ] **A)** A long-term aspirational objective defining the enduring purpose and desired future state of the product.
- [ ] **B)** A detailed specification of all features to be built over the next year.
- [ ] **C)** A short-term goal to be achieved in the next Sprint.
- [ ] **D)** A commitment made by the Development Team at Sprint Planning.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The product vision is a long-term aspirational objective that guides the Scrum Team. It is not a detailed specification, not a Sprint goal, and not a commitment of the Development Team. The Product Owner is accountable for the vision, but it evolves based on feedback.
 
 
</details>

### 12. Which of the following are true about the Product Goal? (Select two.)

- [ ] **A)** It is a commitment of the Product Backlog.
- [ ] **B)** It is set during Sprint Planning.
- [ ] **C)** It describes a future state of the product that serves as a target for the Scrum Team.
- [ ] **D)** It is the same as the Sprint Goal.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, C**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The Product Goal is a commitment of the Product Backlog (Scrum Guide 2020) and describes a future state of the product. It is not set during Sprint Planning; Sprint Planning produces the Sprint Goal. The Product Goal spans multiple Sprints and is distinct from the Sprint Goal.
 
 
</details>

- [ ] **B)** Creating the Sprint Backlog.

### 16. Refer to the code block. Which ordering technique could the Product Owner use to resolve the prioritization conflict?

```text
A Product Owner has a backlog with items: 'payment gateway', 'user profile page', 'export to PDF', and 'dark mode'. Stakeholders argue all are critical for the next release, but the Development Team can only complete about half. The Product Owner needs a quick categorization to set expectations.
```

- [ ] **A)** Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF)
- [ ] **B)** MoSCoW
- [ ] **C)** Kano Model
- [ ] **D)** Value vs. Effort Quadrant

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: B**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The scenario describes a situation where items need to be categorized by necessity for a release. MoSCoW (Must, Should, Could, Won't) is a simple technique for release-level categorization. WSJF requires cost of delay data not provided. Kano and Value vs. Effort are also possible but MoSCoW fits best here.
 
 
</details>

### 17. What is the primary purpose of the Sprint Review?

- [ ] **A)** To inspect the Increment and adapt the Product Backlog.
- [ ] **B)** To demonstrate finished work to stakeholders without discussion.
- [ ] **C)** To review the team's performance and assign tasks for the next Sprint.
- [ ] **D)** To decide which team members will work on which tasks.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The Sprint Review is a working session where the team inspects the Increment and stakeholders provide feedback to adapt the Product Backlog. It is not a demo or performance review. Task assignment happens in Sprint Planning.
 
 
</details>

### 19. Look at the scenario in the code block. What principle of value-driven development is being violated?

```text
A team working on a SaaS product releases a new feature called 'automated invoicing' after two Sprints. The feature is partially implemented: invoices can be generated but not emailed to customers. The team claims this is an MVP and they can finish the email part later. The Product Owner agrees to release it now.
```

- [ ] **A)** Every increment must be potentially shippable and meet the Definition of Done.
- [ ] **B)** The Product Owner must prioritize value over stakeholder demands.
- [ ] **C)** The team must deliver as many features as possible in each Sprint.
- [ ] **D)** Stakeholder feedback should be collected only at the end of the project.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Value-driven development requires that each Increment is 'Done' (meets the Definition of Done) and potentially releasable. The scenario shows the team releasing an incomplete feature as an 'MVP', violating the DoD. An MVP should still be done and valuable.
 
 
</details>

### 20. What is the Definition of Done?

- [ ] **A)** A formal description of the state of the Increment when it meets the quality measures required for the product.
- [ ] **B)** A list of tasks that must be completed before a Sprint ends.
- [ ] **C)** A document that defines when a Product Backlog item is ready for Sprint Planning.
- [ ] **D)** A commitment of the Sprint Backlog.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The Definition of Done is a commitment of the Increment. It is a shared understanding of what it means for work to be complete and potentially usable. It is not a task list, not a 'Ready' definition, and not a Sprint Backlog commitment.
 
 
</details>

- [ ] **A)** Transparency, Inspection, Adaptation

- [ ] **C)** Commitment, Courage, Focus

</details>
