### **Developing People and Teams**

### 1. What does fostering self-management in Scrum primarily mean for Developers?

- [ ] **A)** Developers decide how to turn Product Backlog items into increments of value
- [ ] **B)** The Product Owner directs each developer's daily tasks
- [ ] **C)** The Scrum Master assigns Sprint tasks to Developers
- [ ] **D)** External stakeholders approve task assignments

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Self-management means Developers control their own assignments and technical approaches, with the Product Owner focusing on value and Product Backlog.
 
 
</details>

### 2. Which two behaviors demonstrate a Product Owner fostering self-management?

- [ ] **A)** Trusting Developers' technical expertise and accepting experimentation
- [ ] **B)** Allowing Developers to choose how to perform the work
- [ ] **C)** Assigning specific implementation tasks to individual Developers
- [ ] **D)** Requiring the Product Owner to approve engineering architecture

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, B**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> A Product Owner fosters self-management by trusting Developers and letting them decide the how; assigning tasks and approving architecture violates Scrum principles.
 
 
</details>

### **Understanding and Applying the Scrum Framework**

### 20. According to the Scrum definition, what is Scrum best described as?

- [ ] **A)** A lightweight framework that helps people, teams, and organizations generate value through adaptive solutions for complex problems
- [ ] **B)** A predictive project management methodology with fixed phases and gates
- [ ] **C)** A comprehensive process that dictates every engineering task
- [ ] **D)** A toolset for enforcing Scrum best practices

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Scrum is deliberately lightweight and incomplete. It supports value creation through empirical, adaptive solutions rather than predictive, prescriptive project management.
 
 
</details>

### 21. Which two statements about the pillars of empiricism are true? (Choose two.)

- [ ] **A)** Transparency ensures that significant aspects of the process are visible to those responsible for the outcome.
- [ ] **B)** Inspection is meaningful only when the information inspected is transparent.
- [ ] **C)** Adaptation should be postponed until a complete annual plan is approved.
- [ ] **D)** Predictive planning is the foundation of empirical process control.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, B**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Empiricism requires transparency, inspection, and adaptation. Transparent information makes inspection reliable, and adaptation must happen promptly after inspection reveals deviations.
 
 
</details>
