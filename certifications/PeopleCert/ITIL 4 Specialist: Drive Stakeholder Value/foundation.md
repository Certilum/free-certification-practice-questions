### **Aligning and realigning expectations and agreeing on service outcomes**

### 1. What is the definition of a touchpoint in the context of customer journeys?

- [ ] **A)** A moment of interaction between customer and provider
- [ ] **B)** The technology platform that supports a service
- [ ] **C)** An internal process improvement initiative
- [ ] **D)** A stakeholder's emotional reaction

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Touchpoints are specific moments of interaction, such as service desk calls or portal logins. They do not refer to the technology itself, the provider's internal processes, or emotional reactions.
 
 
</details>

### 2. Which of the following are common traps to avoid when using journey maps?

- [ ] **A)** Confusing service blueprint with customer journey map
- [ ] **B)** Assuming frictionless technical process implies positive journey
- [ ] **C)** Treating journey mapping as a one-time project
- [ ] **D)** Focusing on output rather than outcome

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, B, C**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Journey mapping must not ignore human/emotional elements and should be continuously updated. Focusing on output versus outcome is an orchestration trap, not a journey mapping trap.
 
 
</details>
