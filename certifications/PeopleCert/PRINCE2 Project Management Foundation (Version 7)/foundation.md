### **Principles**

### 1. In a PRINCE2 project, who is ultimately accountable for the validity of the Business Case?

- [ ] **A)** The Executive
- [ ] **B)** The Project Manager
- [ ] **C)** The Project Board
- [ ] **D)** The Change Authority

<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>

> 💡  **Explanation** 
>

</details>
