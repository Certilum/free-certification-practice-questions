### 14. Given the activity durations below, what is the duration of the critical path?

```plain
Activity A: 5 days, dependent on none
Activity B: 4 days, dependent on A
Activity C: 8 days, dependent on A
Activity D: 6 days, dependent on B
Activity E: 5 days, dependent on C
```

- [ ] **A)** 10 days
- [ ] **B)** 15 days
- [ ] **C)** 18 days
- [ ] **D)** 20 days

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: C**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The critical path is the longest sequence: A (5) + C (8) + E (5) = 18 days. Path A-B-D is 4+6+4=14 days.
