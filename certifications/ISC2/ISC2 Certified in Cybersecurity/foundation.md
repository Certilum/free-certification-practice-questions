### **Access Control Concepts**

### 1. Which process verifies that a user is who they claim to be?

- [ ] **A)** Authentication
- [ ] **B)** Authorization
- [ ] **C)** Accounting
- [ ] **D)** Identification

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Authentication verifies the identity claim through factors such as passwords, tokens, or biometrics. It is the first gate before accessing resources.
 
 
</details>

### 2. Which of the following are authentication factors? Select all that apply.
