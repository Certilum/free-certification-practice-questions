### 1. What is the primary purpose of structured analytic techniques (SATs) in threat intelligence?

### **Threat Actor Analysis and Profiling**

### 25. What are the four core components of the Diamond Model of Intrusion Analysis?

- [ ] **A)** Adversary, Capability, Infrastructure, Victim
- [ ] **B)** Adversary, Tool, Network, Target
- [ ] **C)** Attacker, Exploit, Server, Organization
- [ ] **D)** Threat Actor, Malware, Domain, Company

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The Diamond Model defines four vertices: Adversary (threat actor), Capability (tools and malware), Infrastructure (IPs, domains, servers), and Victim (target organization). All other combinations are incorrect because they either rename or omit one of the core components.
 
 
</details>

- [ ] **A)** Reconnaissance
- [ ] **B)** Weaponization
- [ ] **C)** Delivery
- [ ] **D)** Attribution

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, B, C**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The Cyber Kill Chain includes seven phases: Reconnaissance, Weaponization, Delivery, Exploitation, Installation, Command & Control, and Actions on Objectives. 'Attribution' is not a phase; it is an outcome of analysis using frameworks like the Diamond Model.
 
 
</details>
