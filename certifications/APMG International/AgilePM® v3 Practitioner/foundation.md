### **AgilePM Practices and Techniques**

### 1. What is the primary characteristic of a 'Must Have' requirement in a timebox?

- [ ] **A)** It is on the critical path of the schedule.
- [ ] **B)** It is desirable but not vital.
- [ ] **C)** It is critical; without it the solution is unviable.
- [ ] **D)** It is delivered only if time permits.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: C**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> A 'Must Have' is a non-negotiable requirement without which the solution cannot be used. It is not defined by the critical path.
 
 
</details>

### 2. Which of the following are true about the 60% rule for Must Haves? (Select all that apply)

- [ ] **A)** Must Haves must not exceed 60% of effort in any timebox.
- [ ] **B)** The rule ensures timeboxes are not overloaded.
- [ ] **C)** It is a guideline that the Business Sponsor can override.
- [ ] **D)** Must Haves can go above 60% if the team works overtime.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, B**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The 60% rule is mandatory and prevents overcommitment, preserving capacity for Shoulds/Coulds and change.
 
 
</details>
