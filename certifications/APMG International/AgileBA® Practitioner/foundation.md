### **Agile Fundamentals and the AgileBA Framework**

### 1. Which of the following is not one of the four values of the Agile Manifesto?

- [ ] **A)** Individuals and interactions over processes and tools

#### **✅ Correct Answer: D**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The Agile Manifesto states 'Individuals and interactions over processes and tools,' not the reverse.
 
 
</details>

### **Stakeholder Management and People**

### 21. Which stakeholder classification technique uses a 2x2 matrix based on power and interest?

- [ ] **A)** RACI Matrix
- [ ] **B)** Power/Interest Grid
- [ ] **C)** Salience Model
- [ ] **D)** Stakeholder Onion Diagram

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: B**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The Power/Interest Grid classifies stakeholders into four quadrants based on power to influence and interest in project outcomes.
 
 
</details>

### 22. Which two statements about the 'Accountable' role in a RACI matrix are correct?

- [ ] **A)** There can be multiple persons assigned as Accountable for a single task.
- [ ] **B)** The Accountable person is often the Product Owner in agile projects.
- [ ] **C)** The Accountable person is the one who performs the work.
- [ ] **D)** The Accountable person is ultimately answerable for the deliverable.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: B, D**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> In RACI, 'Accountable' is a single person who is ultimately answerable, often the Product Owner. The doer is 'Responsible'.
 
 
</details>

</details>
