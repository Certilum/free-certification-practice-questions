### **Fundamentals and The Philosophy of AgilePM**

### 1. What is the primary origin of the Agile philosophy as used in AgilePM?

- [ ] **A)** Lean manufacturing and iterative software development
- [ ] **B)** Waterfall project management
- [ ] **C)** Six Sigma quality control
- [ ] **D)** PRINCE2 methodology

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Agile originates from Lean manufacturing (Toyota Production System) and iterative software methods like RAD and the Spiral model, later formalised by the Agile Manifesto.
 
 
</details>

### 2. Which two of the following are core values of the Agile Manifesto?

- [ ] **A)** Individuals and interactions over processes and tools
- [ ] **B)** Working software over comprehensive documentation
- [ ] **C)** Customer collaboration over contract negotiation
- [ ] **D)** Responding to change over following a plan

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, D**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The Manifesto values individuals and interactions over processes and tools, and responding to change over following a plan. The other options are not explicit Manifesto values.
 
 
</details>

```text
The Agile Manifesto states: "Welcome changing requirements, even late in development."
```

- [ ] **A)** MoSCoW prioritisation and iterative timeboxing
- [ ] **B)** Fixed project scope at startup
- [ ] **C)** Comprehensive upfront documentation
- [ ] **D)** Single final delivery at project end

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> This Manifesto principle directly supports AgilePM's practice of allowing requirement changes via controlled re-prioritisation within timeboxes.
 
 
</details>

### 4. In AgilePM, what is the primary purpose of using MoSCoW prioritisation?

- [ ] **A)** To allocate effort and prioritise requirements based on business value
- [ ] **B)** To create detailed project schedules
- [ ] **C)** To replace the need for a Business Case
- [ ] **D)** To define the technical architecture of the solution

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> MoSCoW enables the team to deliver maximum business value by managing scope trade-offs within fixed time and cost constraints.
 
 
</details>

### 5. Which two statements correctly describe the rationale for using timeboxing in AgilePM?

- [ ] **A)** It forces the team to focus on delivering the highest-priority items within a fixed period
- [ ] **B)** It allows unlimited scope extension if all work cannot be completed
- [ ] **C)** It replaces the need for any project planning
- [ ] **D)** It enforces disciplined prioritisation and prevents scope creep

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, D**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Timeboxing forces scope negotiation and prevents scope creep by fixing the end date; it is not about completing all tasks regardless of cost nor about eliminating planning.
 
 
</details>

### 6. A project manager states: "We will define all requirements upfront and avoid changes." Which AgilePM principle does this statement violate?

```text
Project Manager: "We will define all requirements upfront and avoid any changes during development."
```

- [ ] **A)** Responding to change over following a plan
- [ ] **B)** Focus on business need
- [ ] **C)** Collaborate over contract negotiation
- [ ] **D)** Deliver on time no matter what

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> AgilePM embraces change through iterative delivery and controlled re-prioritisation; upfront fixed scope contradicts the principle of responding to change.
 
 
</details>

### 7. What is the philosophical difference between "Fit for Purpose" and "Fit for Specification" in AgilePM?

- [ ] **A)** Fit for purpose is more important because it focuses on delivering business value
- [ ] **B)** Fit for specification is always required for compliance
- [ ] **C)** Both are equally important and cannot be traded off
- [ ] **D)** Fit for purpose only applies to the testing phase

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> AgilePM values delivering a solution that meets actual business needs (fit for purpose) over rigidly following an old specification (fit for specification).
 
 
</details>

### **The AgilePM Practices and Techniques**

### 8. In MoSCoW prioritisation, what does "Must Have" mean for a timebox?

- [ ] **A)** A requirement without which the solution fails in the current timebox
- [ ] **B)** A requirement that the customer demands but can be deferred
- [ ] **C)** A requirement that must be completed across the entire project
- [ ] **D)** A requirement that is nice to have if time permits

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> A Must Have is non-negotiable for the timebox; without it the solution would be considered a failure.
 
 
</details>

### 9. Which two statements correctly describe the 60/20/20 rule in relation to timeboxing and MoSCoW?

- [ ] **A)** Must Have items should not exceed 60% of total effort in a timebox
- [ ] **B)** Should Have items can use up to 40% of the effort
- [ ] **C)** Could Have items are allocated exactly 30% of the effort
- [ ] **D)** Should Have and Could Have each have a maximum of 20% effort allocation

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, D**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Must Haves should not exceed 60% of the timebox effort; Should and Could each get up to 20%. This rule ensures scope flexibility.
 
 
</details>

### 10. During a timebox, the team has completed all Must Haves but the deadline is approaching. What should the team do with uncompleted Should Haves?

```text
Timebox status: Must Haves done, Should Haves 50% complete, deadline is tomorrow.
```

- [ ] **A)** Defer the Should Haves to the next timebox and close the current timebox on time
- [ ] **B)** Extend the timebox until all Should Haves are completed
- [ ] **C)** Drop some Must Haves to free up time for Should Haves
- [ ] **D)** Ask the Business Sponsor for a deadline extension

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Should Haves are deferred to the next timebox; the team must deliver on time and cannot extend the current timebox.
 
 
</details>

### 11. What are the three stages of a timebox in AgilePM?

- [ ] **A)** Investigation, Refinement, Consolidation
- [ ] **B)** Plan, Do, Check, Act
- [ ] **C)** Design, Build, Test, Deploy
- [ ] **D)** Feasibility, Foundations, Engineering

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The three stages are Investigation (plan and understand), Refinement (build and test), and Consolidation (final review and acceptance).
 
 
</details>

### 12. Which two statements correctly describe a facilitated workshop in AgilePM?

- [ ] **A)** A timeboxed session with a neutral facilitator that produces a tangible output
- [ ] **B)** An informal meeting without agenda or time constraints
- [ ] **C)** A session where the Project Manager makes all decisions without stakeholder input
- [ ] **D)** A tool to build consensus and shared understanding among stakeholders

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, D**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Workshops are structured, timeboxed sessions led by a neutral facilitator, producing agreed outcomes. They are not open-ended discussions nor do they eliminate modelling.
 
 
</details>

</details>

### 22. Which two roles are empowered to make technical decisions within an AgilePM project?

- [ ] **A)** Deliver on Time
- [ ] **B)** Focus on Business Need

- [ ] **D)** Build Incrementally from Firm Foundations

> 💡  **Explanation** 
>

</details>

- [ ] **A)** Focus on the Business Need
- [ ] **B)** Complete upfront planning
- [ ] **C)** Deliver on Time

<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>

</details>

#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation**

</details>

### 30. Which two statements correctly describe the AgilePM principle "Collaborate"?

- [ ] **A)** Empowered stakeholders and development teams work together daily
- [ ] **B)** The Project Manager makes all final decisions without consultation
- [ ] **C)** Complete consensus is required on every decision

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, D**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
>

</details>
