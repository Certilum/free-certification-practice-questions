### **Communication and Modelling**

### 1. What is the standard format for expressing a User Story in Agile?

- [ ] **A)** As a [user role], I want [goal] so that [reason]
- [ ] **B)** Given [context], When [event], Then [outcome]
- [ ] **C)** Title, Description, Acceptance Criteria, Priority
- [ ] **D)** Actor, Precondition, Main Flow, Postcondition

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The standard user story format is 'As a [user role], I want [goal] so that [reason].' It is a placeholder for conversation and not a full specification. The other options describe other artefacts: Given-When-Then is for Gherkin, the third is a generic story card format, and the fourth is a use case structure.
 
 
</details>

### **Elicitation and Collaboration**

### 7. According to the Power-Interest Grid, what is the appropriate engagement strategy for a stakeholder with high power and low interest?

- [ ] **A)** Manage Closely
- [ ] **B)** Keep Satisfied
- [ ] **C)** Keep Informed
- [ ] **D)** Monitor

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: B**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> According to the Power-Interest Grid, a stakeholder with high power and low interest should be kept satisfied. This means providing sufficient attention to meet their needs without overwhelming them with excessive detail. Over-engaging (Manage Closely) would waste their time, while under-engaging (Keep Informed or Monitor) risks dissatisfaction.
 
 
</details>

### 8. Which of the following are correct statements about workshops as an elicitation technique? (Select two.)

- [ ] **A)** Workshops are best suited for one-on-one discussions on sensitive topics.
- [ ] **B)** A neutral facilitator is essential to manage group dynamics and keep the session on track.
- [ ] **C)** Workshops are typically timeboxed to ensure focused and efficient collaboration.
- [ ] **D)** Workshops always produce a prioritized product backlog as the primary output.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: B, C**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> Workshops require a neutral facilitator to guide discussions and ensure all voices are heard. They are also timeboxed to align with agile principles. Workshops are not ideal for sensitive one-on-one topics (interviews are better) and their outputs can vary (e.g., story maps, decisions) – not always a prioritized backlog.
 
 
</details>

- [ ] **B)** It defines the initial context or preconditions.

### 10. What is the primary purpose of an Information Radiator in an agile project?

- [ ] **A)** To communicate project status exclusively to the project manager.
- [ ] **B)** To passively broadcast key project information to the whole team and stakeholders.
- [ ] **C)** To generate a large quantity of creative ideas during a workshop.
- [ ] **D)** To document detailed acceptance criteria for user stories.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: B**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
>

</details>

### 11. Which two statements correctly describe the RACI matrix in an agile context? (Select two.)

- [ ] **A)** The Business Analyst is typically Accountable for stakeholder satisfaction.
- [ ] **B)** Only one person can be designated as Accountable for a given task or decision.
- [ ] **C)** 'Consulted' indicates two-way communication where input is sought before decisions.
- [ ] **D)** 'Informed' means the stakeholder provides input before a decision is made.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: B, C**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> In RACI, Accountability is singular – only one person is Accountable for each activity. 'Consulted' involves two-way communication; those consulted provide input before decisions. The BA is typically Responsible for facilitating engagement, but the Product Owner is Accountable for stakeholder satisfaction. 'Informed' is one-way notification after decisions.
 
 
</details>

- [ ] **B)** To establish and maintain documented relationships between requirements and their origins, implementation, and verification artifacts

</details>
