### **BRM Foundation and Principles**

### 1. What is the primary purpose of Business Relationship Management (BRM) according to the APMG BRMP syllabus?

- [ ] **A)** To ensure IT projects are delivered on time and within budget.
- [ ] **B)** To align business strategy with IT service delivery and co-create value.
- [ ] **C)** To manage service level agreements and incident tickets.
- [ ] **D)** To act as a helpdesk escalation point for business users.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: B**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> BRM is defined as a strategic discipline that aligns business strategy with IT service delivery, fostering a culture of trust and collaboration to co-create value. The other options describe project management, service level management, or helpdesk functions, which are not the primary purpose of BRM.
 
 
</details>

### 4. Which BRM principle emphasizes that value is jointly realized through collaboration and shared risk?

- [ ] **A)** Reciprocity

### 25. What is the primary distinction between tangible and intangible value in the context of BRM?

- [ ] **A)** Tangible value is financial and measurable; intangible value is qualitative and perceptual
- [ ] **B)** Tangible value is long-term; intangible value is short-term
- [ ] **C)** Tangible value is subjective; intangible value is objective
- [ ] **D)** Tangible value is always measurable in time; intangible value in money

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> According to the playbook, tangible value (hard benefits) is quantifiable and monetary, while intangible value (soft benefits) is qualitative, perceptual, and subjective. The correct distinction is financial/measurable vs. qualitative/perceptual.
 
 
</details>
