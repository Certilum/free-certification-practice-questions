### **Change and the Individual**

### 15. What does the Kübler-Ross Change Curve originally describe?

- [ ] **A)** Emotional stages people go through when facing a significant change
- [ ] **B)** Organizational phases of a change project
- [ ] **C)** Communication strategies for change managers
- [ ] **D)** The steps to implement a new process

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The Kübler-Ross Change Curve, originally developed for grief, describes five emotional stages: denial, anger, bargaining, depression, and acceptance. In change management, it is adapted to understand individual responses to organizational change.
 
 
</details>

> 💡  **Explanation**

</details>

### 18. According to Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory, which of the following is considered a Motivator?

- [ ] **A)** Job security
- [ ] **B)** Salary
- [ ] **C)** Recognition
- [ ] **D)** Work conditions

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: C**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
>
