### **The Management Case**

### 22. Who is ultimately accountable for ensuring the benefits stated in the business case are delivered?

- [ ] **A)** Senior Responsible Owner (SRO)
- [ ] **B)** Project Manager (PM)
- [ ] **C)** Senior User
- [ ] **D)** Senior Supplier

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The SRO is the single accountable individual for the business case and the project outcome, including benefits realisation. The PM is responsible for execution but not accountable for benefits. The Senior User and Senior Supplier are board members with specific representative roles but not overall accountability.
 
 
</details>

### 23. Which of the following roles are typically members of the Project Board according to the Better Business Cases framework? (Select all that apply.)

- [ ] **A)** Senior Responsible Owner (SRO)
- [ ] **B)** Senior User
- [ ] **C)** Senior Supplier
- [ ] **D)** Project Manager

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A, B, C**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The Project Board comprises the SRO (Executive), Senior User, and Senior Supplier. The Project Manager attends as an advisor but is not a voting member of the board.
 
 
</details>

### 24. Consider the following decision logic for a governance approach. Based on the BC guidance, what should the SRO do next when a Gateway review identifies an Amber rating?

```pseudo
IF GatewayReview.Rating == "Red":
    Halt project
ELSE IF GatewayReview.Rating == "Amber":
    Resolve issues
    THEN seek approval
ELSE IF GatewayReview.Rating == "Green":
    Proceed to approval
END IF
```

- [ ] **A)** Proceed to immediate approval as the review is not Red.
- [ ] **B)** Reject the business case and halt the project.
- [ ] **C)** Address the issues identified by the review before seeking approval.

<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: C**
 
> 💡  **Explanation**
