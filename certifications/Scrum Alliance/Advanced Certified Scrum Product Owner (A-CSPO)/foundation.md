### 7. According to the playbook, how does Product Owner mentoring differ from Scrum Master mentoring?

- [ ] **A)** The PO mentors on value; the SM mentors on process.
- [ ] **B)** The PO mentors on process; the SM mentors on value.
- [ ] **C)** The PO mentors on velocity; the SM mentors on ROI.
- [ ] **D)** The PO and SM both focus exclusively on ceremonies.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> The Scrum Master Confusion Trap warns against confusing process-based mentoring with value-based mentoring. The PO mentors on value, market fit, and ROI, while the SM mentors on Scrum ceremonies and velocity.
 
 
</details>
