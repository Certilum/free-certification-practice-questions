### **Product Backlog Management**

### 1. In the INVEST model, what does the 'Valuable' criterion require for a Product Backlog item?

- [ ] **A)** It delivers user benefit.
- [ ] **B)** It has no dependencies.
- [ ] **C)** It is small enough for one Sprint.
- [ ] **D)** It can be verified by tests.

<details>
<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation** 
> 
> 'Valuable' means the story delivers benefit to the user or customer, not that it is small, independent, or testable.
 
 
</details>

</details>
