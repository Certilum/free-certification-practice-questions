### **Governance and Management of IT**

### 1. Which one of the following activities best represents IT governance rather than routine operational management?

- [ ] **A)** Evaluating investment outcomes against strategy
- [ ] **B)** Configuring firewall rules on servers
- [ ] **C)** Executing a planned database migration

<summary> 🔍 Reveal Answer</summary>
   
#### **✅ Correct Answer: A**
 
> 💡  **Explanation**

</details>
